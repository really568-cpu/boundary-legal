Where are my notes stored?

On your device. BOUNDARY does not require an account, and your notes are never uploaded. The only feature that sends anything is AI assistance, and it asks for your permission the first time you use it.

How do I back up my work?

Every thinking session can be exported from within the app. Exported files are saved to your device or shared through the iOS share sheet.

How do I delete my data?

Open Settings in the app and choose Delete All Data. Deleting the app also removes everything stored on your device.

I subscribed but the app still asks me to subscribe

Open the paywall or Settings and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in to the same Apple ID you used to subscribe. If it still does not unlock, email us with your Apple ID email and the approximate purchase date.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. Open Settings → Your Name → Subscriptions on your iPhone, or visit apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

What AI does the app use?

AI assistance uses DeepSeek. When you use it, the document you are working on is sent to our proxy service and then to DeepSeek to generate a response. Your other documents are never sent. You can turn AI assistance off at any time in Settings and keep using every other feature. See our Privacy Policy for details.

AI assistance is not working

Check your internet connection and try again. If the app shows a temporary unavailability message, please try again later — some limits reset daily.

我的笔记存在哪里？

存在你自己的设备上。BOUNDARY 不需要注册账号，笔记不会上传。唯一会把内容发出去的功能是 AI 辅助，首次使用时会先征求你的同意。

怎么导出和备份？

每次思考都可以在 App 内导出，导出后可以保存到设备或通过 iOS 分享。

怎么删除数据？

App 内「设置 → 清空全部数据」。删除 App 也会一并删除设备上的全部内容。

已经订阅了，但 App 还让我订阅

在付费页或设置里点「恢复购买」，并确认登录的是同一个 Apple ID。如果仍然不行，把购买用的 Apple ID 邮箱和大致购买日期发给我们。

怎么取消订阅？

订阅由 Apple 管理：手机「设置 → 你的名字 → 订阅」里可以取消，或访问 apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions。

怎么申请退款？

退款由 Apple 处理，请在 reportaproblem.apple.com 申请。

AI 用的是哪个模型？

AI 辅助使用 DeepSeek。使用时，当前文档会经我们的代理服务转发给 DeepSeek 生成结果，你其他的文档不会被发送。可以随时在设置里关闭 AI，其余功能不受影响。详见隐私政策。

AI 辅助不工作怎么办？

先确认网络正常，再试一次。如果 App 提示服务暂时不可用，请稍后再试——部分额度每天会重置。
